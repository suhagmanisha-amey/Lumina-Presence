## 1. The "94% of senior leaders are open to leaving" report
**Strength: Strong. Timeliness: Excellent (published 9–10 Sept, 4–5 days old)**

**What it is:** A new report covering senior executives in India found 94% report rising career mobility and are open to new roles. Coverage framed the drivers as AI disruption and career-growth hunger — one outlet headlined it as AI and growth ambitions fuelling executive job-hopping.

**Why it matters to *this* audience specifically:** This is the single most on-brand data point of the week. Lumina's audience isn't people who lack ability — it's people whose ability isn't being *recognised*. A stat saying 94% of senior leaders are mentally halfway out the door is the statistical shadow of exactly that feeling: "I'm doing the work and it isn't landing, so I'll go somewhere it might." The brand's contrarian angle writes itself and avoids competitor callouts entirely: *changing rooms doesn't fix being unheard in the room.* If your ideas get ignored until someone else repeats them, a new company just relocates the problem. That reframe positions the Lumina Method as the alternative to the resignation letter — and it's a far better hook than generic "build your confidence" content.

**Why this week:** The report dropped within the last five days and is being picked up across multiple outlets simultaneously, so the number is in circulation right now. It also lands squarely in interview/mobility season for senior roles — this is when people are actually acting on it, not just feeling it.
